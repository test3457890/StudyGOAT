Start with Learn, then Practice and Play. Open Sources & coverage before using a track. Review Mistakes after a quiz. Practice Test draws ten distinct questions from the available slice, not a full certification blueprint.

Source check dates do not establish full objective coverage or provider approval. Confirm the current exam code and official requirements before booking. Security+ current-version verification remains open. WFA needs the learner's provider syllabus and clinical review. Technician needs the corrected NCVEC pool and diagrams. CAP practice never awards official credit or uses controlled milestone material.

See the individual track pages and Roadmap for gaps. Do not upload confidential employer documents, exam dumps or restricted training materials.
