# StudyGOAT for Students

StudyGOAT helps students study a topic at a time rather than relying on one long review session.

## Suggested study routine

1. Choose the course you need to work on.
2. Open Learn and review one topic.
3. Use Practice to test your understanding.
4. Open Mistakes and correct answers you missed.
5. Use Play for a faster review of definitions.
6. Try Mixed Quiz or Exam after covering several topics.

## Before a class exam

- Check the professor's slides, assignments, and exam scope first.
- Focus on topics you do not yet understand.
- Use worked examples, not only memorized answers.
- Retry missed questions after reviewing the method.
- Confirm the application's notation matches your course.

## When creating a course

Custom courses can be built from supported source materials when an AI provider is connected. Upload only materials you are allowed to use or share with that provider. Review generated lessons and answers carefully.

## What to track

Pay attention to the topics you miss repeatedly, not only XP or game scores. A shorter review of a weak topic may help more than another game round on material you already know.

## Academic integrity

Use StudyGOAT to understand material and practice. Follow your school's rules for graded assignments, quizzes, and exams.
