## Driving coverage

Arizona and New Jersey have selected practice. The in-app directory includes all 50 states and DC; the other 48 states and DC are explicitly planned, with no lessons or questions. Every additional jurisdiction needs its own official manual verification, revision record, state-specific lessons, original questions and review. Arizona rules are never substituted for another state. Territories are outside this initial directory and remain future work.

New Jersey uses the official MVC manual with a 2026 cover. Selected sections are chapter 4, printed pages 67-68 (intersections), 72 (turns), and 77 (school buses). Full manual SHA-256 is stored in the source register data. This is not an official MVC question bank.
