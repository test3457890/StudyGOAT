# Arizona driving prep

First state module, published in the November 9, 2026 expansion. Arizona only. Six lessons, 30 original questions and 12 glossary cards cover selected signs, signals, right-of-way, crossings and permit preparation. Three original SVG sign-shape illustrations support visual practice.

## Official sources and limits

- [Arizona Driver License Manual](https://apps.azdot.gov/files/mvd/mvd-forms-lib/99-0117.pdf), revised September 2026. Downloaded PDF revision verified directly; SHA-256 retained in the structured source record. Selected printed pages 35 to 37 and 40 to 42.
- [ADOT permit-test information](https://azdot.gov/mvd/services/driver-license-ID/permit-test), Preparation section, checked November 9, 2026. Official test: 30 multiple-choice questions, at least 80 percent to pass.

Some web-reader results displayed an older manual revision. The downloaded September PDF was used for this content. Refer to current statutes and ADOT guidance when requirements change.

StudyGOAT questions and sign shapes are original educational material. No official test bank or ADOT illustration is reproduced. The short ten-question practice test samples this partial bank; it is not a full Arizona exam simulation. Study the entire official manual. The application grants no permit, licence, training completion or road-test credit.

## How to study

Choose Arizona Driving Prep on the main menu. Learn the rules and scenarios, then use Practice, Play and Mistakes. Open Sources & coverage to see scope and references. These rules must not be treated as a substitute for another state's manual.
