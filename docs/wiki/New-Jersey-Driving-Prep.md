# New Jersey driving prep

Updated November 10, 2026: six lessons and 24 original questions from selected sections of the official 2026 New Jersey MVC Driver Manual.

Covers controlled/uncontrolled intersections, multi-way stops, failed signals, visibility, red-light right turns, signaling and school-bus situations with their specific exceptions. References include printed pages 67-68, 72 and 77. The manual download and hash were checked directly.

[Official MVC manual page](https://www.nj.gov/mvc/about/manuals.htm) · [Official manual PDF](https://www.nj.gov/mvc/pdf/license/drivermanual.pdf)

This is partial study content, not official MVC questions or full test preparation. Licensing/GDL requirements, the remaining rules, signs, signals, road markings, hazards and road-test preparation remain open. No permit or practical credit is awarded. Select New Jersey in the in-app driving directory; do not use Arizona rules for New Jersey.
