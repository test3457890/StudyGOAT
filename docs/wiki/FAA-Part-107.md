# FAA Part 107 drone preparation

Updated November 13, 2026. The course has 47 lessons and 182 original questions. It remains partial UAG preparation, with a complete reading inventory of all 176 published FAA-S-ACS-10B knowledge codes, including lettered sub-elements, across 17 tasks.

## ACS lookup

Choose Browse all 176 ACS knowledge codes from the FAA course. Search an exact code from a Knowledge Test Report, a task prefix or subject text. Exact knowledge codes show only that entry. Availability filters distinguish 75 codes with related partial lessons from 101 codes with official reading only.

Every entry links to its page in the official ACS. Related lesson buttons refer to selected topic material. They do not establish complete knowledge-element coverage, and every question in a related topic does not necessarily test every linked code. Official-reading-only entries currently have no mapped StudyGOAT lesson. Risk-management fields are reserved and skills are not applicable in this knowledge-test ACS; the inventory is not a physical flight-skills assessment.

## Chart exercises

Choose Practice FAA Figure 59 for six original chart-reading questions using the published Toledo, Ohio, sectional excerpt from FAA-CT-8080-2H. Read Class C ceiling/floor labels, surface floors, airport elevation and obstacle MSL/AGL values. Enlarge chart for reading opens a scrollable full-size view; a text description supplies equivalent chart facts.

The figure is historical testing material from the 2018 supplement. It is not to scale and is not for navigation, current airspace determination or flight authorization. The chart is an FAA source image, not a generated substitute. It and the ACS inventory are embedded in the standalone export for offline study.

## Other selected material

Existing rules, weather, loading, performance, Remote ID, night, operations over people/vehicles, reporting, preflight and waiver lessons remain. New material adds chart symbols and vertical references, temporary/special-use restrictions, airport information and traffic listening, contingency preparation, and crew fitness. No lesson authorizes aviation-band transmission. Aircraft-specific lost-link and navigation behavior must come from the actual aircraft documentation; a generic return-to-home assumption is not a universal emergency procedure.

Use current operational charts, notices, aircraft instructions and any required authorizations for an actual flight. Broader ACS instruction, weather/chart applications, Remote ID/category compliance, operational exercises, full-exam blueprint validation and independent subject-matter review remain unfinished. StudyGOAT does not grant an FAA certificate, TRUST completion or CAP sUAS qualification.
