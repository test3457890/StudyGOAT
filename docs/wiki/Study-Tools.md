# Study Tools

StudyGOAT combines learning, practice, and review in one browser-based program. Availability may vary by course and configuration.

## Learn

Follow topic-based lessons with short explanations, worked examples, and step-by-step guidance. Some lessons include prediction questions and interactive examples.

## Practice and Mixed Quiz

Practice a selected topic or answer questions across multiple topics. The built-in courses include generated questions that can vary between rounds.

## Exam

Use a dedicated exam practice mode with configurable question selections and supported question types. Practice exams are study aids, not official or proctored certification exams.

## Quiz Prep

Organize review around an upcoming quiz or exam and choose the topics you want to study.

## Mistakes and Weakest Topic

Return to missed questions and focus on areas that need more practice. These tools help guide review, but they are not a formal assessment of competency.

## Play

The source code includes five study game modes:

- Flashcards: flip cards and rate how well you know each one.
- Learn: adaptive recall rounds with multiple-choice and typing steps.
- Match: pair terms with definitions against a timer.
- Blitz: answer a series of timed questions.
- Lightning: decide quickly whether a term and definition match.

## Additional learning tools

The application also includes topic summaries, progress and accuracy indicators, and optional AI tutoring and test review features when an AI provider is connected.

## Accessibility and study settings

StudyGOAT has settings for adjusting how content is presented. Explore font, spacing, and other available preferences in Settings to find a comfortable reading experience.

## What these tools do not promise

Generated or user-built content can contain errors. Verify important explanations and answers against class materials or official certification objectives. High scores in StudyGOAT do not prove mastery or guarantee a passing exam result.

See [Getting Started](https://github.com/test3457890/StudyGOAT/wiki/Getting-Started) for a quick walkthrough.


## Sourced track practice

New tracks use fixed original questions with checked source sections. Their Practice Test uses ten distinct questions and an optional timer, rather than the university course's mixed-format test builder. No full certification exam simulation is enabled. Question explanations include source links and stable question IDs. Source review dates and missing coverage are available in Sources & coverage.


## Navigation and coverage

Choose Classes, Certifications or Driving Tests, then use the course or state search. FAA study is Part 107 drone preparation. University and custom courses keep their existing tools. The CPSC 222 class includes a finite-state-machine lab.
